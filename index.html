About

One person, the whole chain

Most climate projects pass through several hands: someone cleans the data, someone models it, someone builds the tool, someone briefs the council. I do all four, so the story stays straight from the first dataset to the final decision.

Architect by training, planner by degree, climate risk specialist by practice.

See my full timeline →

  1. 8 years across county and city governments, clean energy companies, and national planning programs.
  2. Registered architect with the Council of Architecture, India, and LEED Green Associate.
  3. MSc Sustainability Management, Columbia University. Built a climate risk and scenario analysis curriculum there.
  4. Public tools in daily use across Broward County for heat, flood and resilience funding.
  5. Trained 50+ Indian cities in climate action planning with NIUA and GIZ.

How I work

Same data, four steps, one decision

The same four steps work for a single building, a city, a county or a whole country. Here, one set of places goes from raw data to the three to act on first.

Swipe through the four steps →

1 · DATA

Collect and clean

Hydrology, health, satellite and emissions data, checked and documented.

2 · ANALYSIS

Model the risk

Every place scored under future scenarios, from low to high risk.

3 · TOOLS

Make it visible

Dashboards and maps that officials and residents actually use.

4 · DECISION

Get it funded

A short list to protect first, ready for a grant or a budget.
