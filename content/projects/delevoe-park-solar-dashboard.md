## The question (one or two sentences)
Broward County runs a 199 kW solar installation at Delevoe Park. How can residents see what it produces, what it saves and how much carbon it avoids?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Built a public, real-time dashboard that pulls in live solar generation data.
- Analysed production trends to measure cost savings, emissions avoided and system performance.
- Set up automated data updates, so the numbers stay accurate without manual work.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
A live public tool that explains the system's output, savings and emissions cuts in plain terms for non-technical readers.
