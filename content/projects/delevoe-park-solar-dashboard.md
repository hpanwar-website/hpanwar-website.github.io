## The question (one or two sentences)
Broward County runs a 199 kW solar installation at Delevoe Park. How can the county see what it produces, what it saves and how much carbon it avoids?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Built a Power BI dashboard that pulls in live solar generation data.
- Analysed production trends to measure cost savings, emissions avoided and system performance.
- Set up automated data updates, so the numbers stay accurate without manual work.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
An internal tool that shows the system's output, savings and emissions cuts in plain terms. The system has saved about $92,000 over four years.
