## The question (one or two sentences)
NITI Aayog, India's national planning body, wanted eco-tourism to grow in Lakshadweep, where the islands and lagoons are small and fragile. Where can building happen, how many visitors can each island hold, and what rules should apply?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Ran GIS land suitability studies, overlaying maps to find zones fit for building, eco-tourism and marine use.
- Prepared a carrying capacity study for the islands and lagoons, based on social, ecological and infrastructure limits.
- Wrote development control regulations for the five project islands, and guidelines for water sports, boating, diving, beaches, tourist behaviour, and beach and water villas.
- Helped prepare feasibility reports and DPRs for nine public-private eco-tourism projects.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
Concept and master plans for a USD 90 million island development package, with clear limits and rules for eco-tourism.
