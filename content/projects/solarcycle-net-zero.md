## The question (one or two sentences)
SOLARCYCLE recycles retired solar panels. What is the real carbon footprint of its products and operations, how does recycling compare with other end-of-life options, and which emission cuts should it make first?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Ran cradle-to-grave life cycle assessments (LCAs) for several types of solar panel.
- Built the company's greenhouse gas inventory, and comparative LCAs to improve the recycling process.
- Built a marginal abatement cost curve (MACC) and pre-feasibility checks, to rank cuts by carbon saved, cost and return.
- Wrote the company-wide net-zero strategy and roadmap.
- Co-authored a published white paper comparing panel reuse with advanced recycling.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
A company-wide net-zero roadmap, and the white paper Reuse vs. Recycling: A Life Cycle Assessment of Solar Panel End-of-Life Pathways (September 2026). It found that advanced recycling into new, higher-efficiency panels can have a lower climate impact than reusing old panels.
