## The question (one or two sentences)
Kavaratti, the capital of Lakshadweep, is a small, eco-sensitive coral island with a USD 65 million smart city plan. How do you deliver projects like waste management and clean beaches without harming the island?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Worked on site as project coordinator: team coordination, client meetings, and shaping and visualising projects.
- Assessed the solid waste chain across 10 Lakshadweep islands, including the market for recyclables, pricing, and the rules on waste, coastal zones and producer responsibility.
- Prepared feasibility reports, detailed project reports, cost estimates and the tender for solid waste, and helped with procurement.
- Supported Blue Flag certification for four beaches: reviewed gaps, operations and staffing, and helped revise and evaluate tenders.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
Waste management and Blue Flag beach projects taken from idea to tender, within a USD 65 million smart city program.
