## The question (one or two sentences)
Cities are asked to protect biodiversity, but most have no simple way to measure it, record it or plan for it. What would a practical toolkit for a city look like?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Co-developed the toolkit with Prof. Meenakshi Dhote at SPA New Delhi's Design Innovation Centre.
- Designed a GIS process that layers satellite imagery, biotope, terrain and land-use maps to classify habitats and pick priority areas.
- Designed an app that brings it together for city bodies, researchers, NGOs and residents, including a way for people to volunteer with local NGOs.
- Surveyed and mapped green cover, tree types, habitats and ecological networks in four Indian cities: Kohima, Pune, Delhi and Agra.
- Built the web portal and mobile app myself, plus the posters, brochures and interactive maps.
- Presented the toolkit for India at the UN desertification conference (UNCCD COP 14, New Delhi, 2019). It won the Best Biodiversity Innovation Award at the India International Science Festival in 2020.
