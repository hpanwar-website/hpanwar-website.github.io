## The question (one or two sentences)
A township was proposed near Delhi University, and the case reached India's National Green Tribunal. What pollution would it add, and how could it be managed?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Identified pollution sources around the site, estimated traffic density and activity, and measured emission loads nearby.
- Mapped activities across a 2 km by 2 km area around the proposed development in GIS.
- Projected emission loads after development, taking weather conditions into account.
- Wrote a feasibility report with preventive management practices.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
An evidence-based assessment, submitted to the National Green Tribunal, with practical steps to limit the township's pollution. The tribunal did not approve the project.
