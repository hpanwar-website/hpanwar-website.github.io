## The question (one or two sentences)
CalPERS, the largest US public pension fund, wanted to understand how physical and transition climate risks affect workers, and through them, company performance.

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Analysed how physical climate risks (like heat and floods) and transition risks (like new policy and markets) affect workforce resilience and long-term company performance.
- Linked climate risk exposure to investment performance and sustainability outcomes.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
Applied research showing an institutional investor why people and workforce belong in its climate risk analysis.
