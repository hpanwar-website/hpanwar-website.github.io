## The question (one or two sentences)
When streets flood, some roads become too deep to drive. Which roads stay passable, and what is the safest route from a flooded neighbourhood to the nearest shelter?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Checked the whole county road network and marked a road as impassable where flood water is deeper than two feet.
- Found the evacuation corridors that stay open.
- Worked out the best routes from flood-affected areas to nearby emergency shelters.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
County-wide maps of safe routes and shelter access that support emergency planning and public safety decisions.

## My role
GIS analysis and route mapping

## Tools and data
ArcGIS Pro, county road network, flood depth data
