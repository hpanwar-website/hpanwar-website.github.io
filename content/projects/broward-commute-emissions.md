## The question (one or two sentences)
Employee commuting is part of the county's Scope 3 emissions, but it had never been measured from real data. How far do staff travel each day, what does that add up to, and how would more electric cars change it?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Mapped where about 8,000 employees live and work in GIS, and worked out each person's daily commuting distance.
- Calculated the emissions from those trips for the Scope 3 part of the county's greenhouse gas inventory.
- Built future scenarios by vehicle type (gas, diesel and electric), with different rates of EV adoption.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
The county's first data-based estimate of employee commuting emissions for its Scope 3 inventory. Under the highest EV adoption scenario, commuting emissions fall by 35%.
