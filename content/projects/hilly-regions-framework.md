## The question (one or two sentences)
India's hill towns face landslides, floods and fast, unplanned growth, and most planning rules were written for the plains. NITI Aayog set up an expert committee to recommend a better way to plan and build in hilly regions.

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Studied how hill settlements are planned today, and the main problems with how they are growing.
- Reviewed the policies, guidelines and building rules that apply to hilly areas.
- Proposed a planning method that maps land suitability, vulnerability, disaster risk and socio-economic factors together.
- Recommended reviving local building traditions and alternative building methods, and set out the governance and institutions needed.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
Input to the expert committee's report to NITI Aayog on a planning and architectural framework for India's hilly regions.
