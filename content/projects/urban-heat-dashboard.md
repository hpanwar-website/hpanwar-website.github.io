## The question (one or two sentences)
People see static maps of surface temperature but can't tell what they mean for their own block, school or clinic. How do you turn heat data into something residents and agencies can explore, and use to decide where to act first?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Designed and built an interactive public dashboard that lets anyone zoom in from the whole county to their own neighbourhood.
- Layered on the county's critical facilities (schools, libraries, clinics and community centres) so agencies can see which ones sit in the hottest areas.
- Added tools that help decide where shade trees, other nature-based solutions or shading structures should go first, to protect the most vulnerable people.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
A live public tool that links heat data to real places, so the county and its partners can see which facilities and communities to help first.
