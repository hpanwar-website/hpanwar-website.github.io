## The question (one or two sentences)
Broward's resilience plan, prepared with the engineering firm Hazen, estimated how much flood damage the planned projects would avoid, by neighbourhood and by industry. The first dashboard was hard to navigate, so most people could not find the numbers that mattered to them.

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Reviewed the original Power BI dashboard delivered under the county's contract with Hazen, and listed where users got lost.
- Redesigned the layout, navigation and charts in Power BI so a resident, a city official or a funder can find their area's numbers quickly.
- Rebuilt the dashboard on the same data, so the figures did not change, only how clearly they read.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
The live public dashboard is my redesigned version. It shows the avoided flood damages from the resilience plan, by neighbourhood and industry, in a cleaner and easier layout.

## My role
Redesign of the dashboard's UI/UX in Power BI

Power BI, Resilient Broward Plan economic data (from Hazen)
