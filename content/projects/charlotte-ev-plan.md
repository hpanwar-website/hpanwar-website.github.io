## The question (one or two sentences)
Charlotte applied for a competitive grant of about USD 1 million to expand EV charging. Where are chargers missing, and which communities are left out?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Mapped existing chargers to check coverage, use and gaps in access.
- Estimated current and future EV demand from population, land-use and transport data, matched to the grant's scoring criteria.
- Picked priority sites for new chargers based on demand gaps, equity and access.
- Made funder-ready maps and analysis for the application.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
The spatial evidence and maps behind a competitive grant application of about USD 1 million, focused on fair access to charging.
