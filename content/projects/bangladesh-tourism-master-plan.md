## The question (one or two sentences)
Bangladesh wants tourism to grow, but much of the country is low-lying, flood-prone and ecologically sensitive. Where can tourism grow safely, and what rules should guide it?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Helped prepare tourism planning frameworks at national, regional and local levels.
- Ran GIS-based climate change and disaster risk vulnerability assessments for tourism areas.
- Mapped environmentally sensitive areas and recommended land-use controls to protect them.
- Built an inventory of tourist destinations, ranked them against performance benchmarks, and set out a framework for tourism clusters, circuits and trails.
- Reviewed the rules and environmental plans for protected and ecologically critical areas, to find where eco-tourism can work.
- Drafted environmental and social safeguards for tourism, and helped run national consultations and field focus groups.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
A national tourism master plan, adopted by the government, from a USD 2 million consulting assignment guiding an investment pipeline of about USD 1.08 billion.
