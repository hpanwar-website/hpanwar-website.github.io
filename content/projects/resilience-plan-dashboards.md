## The question (one or two sentences)
Broward's resilience plan, prepared with the engineering firm Hazen, came with two Power BI dashboards. One shows how much flood damage the plan's projects would avoid, by neighbourhood and industry. The other shows what the projects cost, by year and place. Both had the right numbers, but they were hard to navigate. How do you make them easy for residents, cities and funders to use?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Reviewed both dashboards delivered under the county's contract with Hazen, and listed where users got lost.
- Redesigned the layout, navigation, filters and charts, so a resident, a city official or a funder can find their area's numbers quickly.
- Rebuilt both dashboards on the same data, so the figures did not change, only how clearly they read.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
Both live public dashboards are my redesigned versions. Staff and the public welcomed them as clearer and more professional than the originals.
