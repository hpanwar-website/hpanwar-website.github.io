## The question (one or two sentences)
Companies now have to show how climate change could affect their business. How do you teach students to do that with real numbers, not just frameworks?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Built a climate risk and scenario analysis curriculum at Columbia University, including all of the technical content.
- Wrote every exercise around real cases from different markets and regions.
- Focused the cases on corporate and industrial clients, mostly manufacturers.
- Used Monte Carlo analysis throughout, so students work with ranges and uncertainty, not single numbers.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
A complete curriculum that takes students from climate scenarios to the numbers a company can act on.
