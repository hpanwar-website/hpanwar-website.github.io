## The question (one or two sentences)
The resilience plan, prepared with Hazen, priced the adaptation projects by year and location. The first dashboard showed the data but was hard to use. How do you make billions of dollars of costs readable for cities and the public?

## What I did (3 to 5 points)
- Reviewed the original Power BI dashboard delivered under the county's contract with Hazen.
- Redesigned the layout, filters and navigation in Power BI, so people can see costs by year and by place without hunting.
- Kept the same underlying data, so the redesign changed how clearly the numbers read, not the numbers themselves.

## Result (what changed; a number helps)
The live public dashboard is my redesigned version. It shows the plan's adaptation costs by year and location in a clearer, easier layout.

## My role
Redesign of the dashboard's UI/UX in Power BI
