A 100-year vision for the land around the Taj Mahal

Vision Document, Taj Trapezium Zone · At SPA Delhi, for the Taj Trapezium Committee, Government of Uttar Pradesh · 2018

The Taj Trapezium Zone covers 10,400 sq km around the Taj Mahal, where air and water pollution threaten the monument. How should the region grow over the next 100 years while protecting its environment and heritage?

What I did

  • Assessed the main sources of air and water pollution around the Taj Mahal complex, and ways to reduce them.
  • Mapped natural and tourism resources in GIS, and analysed decades of growth and change in the region's cities.
  • Helped write the vision document and its environmental action plan.
  • Prepared development guidelines (SOPs) for the whole 10,400 sq km zone.
  • Helped run consultations with government departments, private stakeholders and field-level focus groups.
Result

A long-term vision and development guidelines for the Taj Trapezium Zone, a protected zone set up under Supreme Court orders. The vision document was prepared for, and submitted to, the TTZ Board.
